Matri-Kavach Privacy Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Matri-Kavach is a student prototype built by team InnoVerse for Health-a-thon 2026. It helps a nurse find a hospital that is ready for a maternal emergency and tells the hospital, the ambulance and the doctor. It is assistive and never diagnostic.

What data we use

The prototype runs on synthetic data only. No real patient, hospital or family is involved. When a team member tests it on WhatsApp, we receive that test phone number and the text of the messages sent to the Matri-Kavach number, so that the system can reply.

How we use it

Messages are used only to run the referral demo: to read the nurse's or hospital's reply and to send the next step. We do not sell, share or use this data for advertising.

Where it is stored

The demo state is kept in a database on our hosting provider (Render) and is reset regularly. Test data is deleted when the hackathon ends.

Data deletion

To delete any data linked to your phone number, message the Matri-Kavach WhatsApp number with the words "Delete my data", or contact team InnoVerse through Health-a-thon 2026. We delete it within 7 days.
